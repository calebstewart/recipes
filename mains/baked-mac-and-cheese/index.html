Baked Mac and Cheese

Casserole-style mac and cheese: homemade semolina macaroni in a cheddar, Gruyère, and Parmesan béchamel, baked under buttered panko and Parm until it bubbles at the edges. It's built around a full batch of extruded semolina pasta, which you can bake fresh, partly dried, or fully dried. The parboil is judged by feel, not by a time, so it works at any stage. Press the pasta the day before if you can. Fills a 9x13 dish; best eaten the day it's baked.

Instructions

  1. Rack in the center, oven to 375°. Butter a 9x13 baking dish. Put a large pot of well-salted water on to boil.
  2. Toss the panko with the melted butter and half the Parmesan for the topping until evenly coated. Set aside.
  3. Melt 6 Tbsp. butter in a large, heavy pot over medium. Whisk in the flour and cook, whisking, 2 min, until it smells toasty but hasn't browned.
  4. Whisk in the warm milk a cup at a time, letting each addition smooth out before the next. Simmer, whisking often and scraping the corners, 8–10 min, until it coats the back of a spoon. It should be thinner than you'd want to eat, because the pasta keeps drinking sauce in the oven.
  5. Off the heat, whisk in the mustard, cayenne, nutmeg, salt, and pepper. Stir in the cheddar, Gruyère, and Parmesan a handful at a time, letting each melt before adding the next. Taste. It should be a touch saltier than tastes right on its own.
  6. Shake the loose semolina off the pasta and drop it into the boiling water. Parboil by feel, not by the clock: start tasting after 1 min and pull it when it bends but still has a firm, chalky-white core you can see when you bite it. That's roughly half its usual cooking time, so about 1–2 min fresh, 2–4 min partly dried, and 4–5 min fully dried. The oven finishes it.
  7. Drain well; don't rinse. Fold the pasta gently into the sauce with a spatula, which keeps the tubes from tearing. It should look loose and soupy, with sauce pooling around the pasta. If it doesn't, loosen it with a splash of milk.
  8. Spread into the dish. Scatter the topping evenly, then the rest of the topping Parmesan.
  9. Bake 20–25 min, until bubbling at the edges and the panko is deep golden. If the sauce is bubbling but the top is still pale, broil 1–2 min, watching it the whole time.
  10. Rest 10 min before serving so the sauce sets.

Notes

  • Dry the pasta at least partly. Fully fresh tubes work, but they're soft and fragile from an eggless dough. They overcook easily over a 20-minute bake, can collapse when folded into a heavy sauce, and give off more starch, which can make the sauce gluey. Pasta left on the sheet pans for a few hours, or overnight, firms up on the outside and holds up much better. It doesn't need to be dry all the way through for baking. The extruded pasta recipe's warning about mold only applies to sealing damp pasta for storage.
  • The parboil time for partly dried pasta depends on how long it sat out and how humid the kitchen is, which is why step 6 goes by feel. Bite a tube: a thin ring of white in the middle means pull it now. It will look underdone, and it should.
  • Shred the cheese from blocks. Pre-shredded cheese is coated in anti-caking starch that turns the sauce grainy.
  • Add the cheese off the heat. A boiling béchamel can make cheddar break and turn greasy.
  • Warm milk goes into the roux with fewer lumps than cold. A minute or two in the microwave is enough.
  • Make the sauce before the pasta goes in the water, so the drained pasta goes straight into the sauce instead of sitting in the colander and sticking together.
  • The salt is for Diamond Crystal kosher salt. Morton is about twice as salty by volume, so use half as much. The cheeses are salty already, so taste before adding more.
  • Leftovers: the pasta soaks up the sauce in the fridge, so it reheats firmer. Reheat portions covered in a 350° oven or the microwave with a spoonful of milk stirred in. It keeps 3–4 days.
  • Nutrition assumes whole milk, unsalted butter, 1 tsp. Diamond Crystal salt in the sauce, and a little salt absorbed from the pasta water. It's calculated from the extruded semolina pasta nutrition figures.
