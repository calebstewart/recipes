# Crockpot Autumn Harvest Turkey Chili

Ground turkey chili with butternut squash and pumpkin purée, warmed up with
pumpkin pie spice next to the usual chili powder and cumin. It's thick and a
little sweet, and you serve it over rice or quinoa. Brown everything on the
stove first, then leave it in the slow cooker all afternoon.

## Ingredients

- 2 tsp. olive oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 3 bell peppers, any color, seeded and chopped
- 1 jalapeño, chopped (optional)
- 2 lb. ground turkey
- 2 cups raw butternut squash or pumpkin, chopped
- 1 can (28 oz.) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (28 oz.) diced tomatoes, drained
- 1 can (15 oz.) 100% pumpkin purée (not pie filling)
- 2–3 cups mixed canned beans, rinsed and drained
- 1½ tsp. pumpkin pie spice
- 1–2 Tbsp. chili powder
- 1 Tbsp. ground cumin
- 1 tsp. garlic powder
- 1 tsp. dried oregano
- 1 tsp. kosher salt
- Hot sauce, to taste (optional)

### To serve

- 4 cups cooked brown rice or quinoa
- Shredded cheddar or Mexican-blend cheese
- Plain Greek yogurt
- Chopped bell pepper
- Fresh cilantro or parsley

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook the onion, bell peppers, and jalapeño until soft.
2. Add the turkey and cook until browned. Drain the fat.
3. Move the turkey and vegetables to the slow cooker. Add everything else except the hot sauce and stir.
4. Cook on High for 1 hour, then on Low for about 5 hours. Or cook on Low the whole time for 6–7 hours.
5. Taste, then add hot sauce, salt, and more chili powder as needed.
6. Serve over rice or quinoa with whatever toppings you like.

## Notes

- Ground beef works in place of the turkey.
- The original doesn't give a serving count. Two pounds of meat with 4 cups of rice comes to about 8 servings.
- Slow cookers run at different temperatures, so start checking yours toward the short end of the time range.
- Set the toppings out separately and let everyone build their own bowl.
- The nutrition values are estimated from USDA-style figures for each ingredient, since the source doesn't list any. They assume 93/7 ground turkey (fat not drained off), the middle of each range (1½ Tbsp. chili powder, 2½ cups beans), regular canned tomatoes and beans with the beans rinsed, Diamond Crystal kosher salt, and unsalted brown rice. Toppings aren't included.
